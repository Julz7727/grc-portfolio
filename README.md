# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment — Sample Portfolio

A sample risk assessment for a fictitious small e-commerce business (ShopNow), built to demonstrate practical application of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 risk management methodology.

## What's in this repository

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `ShopNow_Risk_Register.xlsx` | Full risk register with 8 risks, scored on a 5×5 likelihood/impact matrix, with inherent and residual ratings, treatment plans, and ISO 27001 Annex A domain mapping |
| `ShopNow_Management_Presentation.pptx` | 10-slide executive presentation summarising findings, methodology, treatment strategy, and decisions required from management |
| `GRC_Three_Concepts_Reference.docx` | Reference notes on Defence in Depth, Security by Design, and the Three Lines Model |

## Methodology

Risks were identified by analysing the case organisation across three lenses: technology stack (website, workstations, payment processing), threat actor (external, opportunistic, insider), and existing incident history. Each risk was scored on a 5×5 qualitative matrix with rating bands derived from the matrix lookup (not strict score addition), and mapped to relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls.

Treatment strategies follow the four ISO 27005 options: Mitigate, Accept, Transfer, Avoid. Every treated risk has a documented residual rating to demonstrate that controls actually reduce exposure.

## Key findings

The fictitious organisation showed 1 Very High, 3 High, and 4 Medium inherent risks. Treatment plans bring residual ratings down to 5 Medium and 3 Low. The standout urgent item is weak password practices (rated 25/25), addressed through MFA, password manager rollout, and policy.

## About this work

This is a learning artifact, not real client work. ShopNow is a fictitious organisation. The methodology and structure mirror what is used in industry risk assessments aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the IIA's Three Lines Model.
